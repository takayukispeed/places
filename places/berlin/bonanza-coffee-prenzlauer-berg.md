## Notes

The original Bonanza café ("Bonanza Coffee Heroes"). Bonanza is named among the top specialty cafés in [Berlin Tourism's coffee guide](https://www.berlintourism.org/berlin-coffee-cafes/). Small local roaster with two cafés, both listed.
