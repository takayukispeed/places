# Klunkerkranich

## Hours

## Notes

Rooftop garden and culture venue on the Neukölln Arcaden parking deck. Seasonal: it closes for the winter, and its 2025 season started on 28 February ([Berliner Zeitung](https://www.berliner-zeitung.de/article/neukoelln-klunkerkranich-geht-bis-ende-februar-in-die-winterpause-2287336)). Under-18s are admitted only with parents from 16:00. Listed among [The Culture Trip's coolest Berlin rooftop bars](https://theculturetrip.com/europe/germany/articles/the-coolest-rooftop-bars-in-berlin).

- Hours: [visitBerlin](https://www.visitberlin.de/en/node/2273979), October 2026; listed as Mon–Wed: Closed; Thu–Sun: 16:00–late (no closing time published). Hours change with the season and events. Top10 Berlin (July 2026) says the gates open at 17:00 on weekdays.
