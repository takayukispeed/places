## Notes

Beer garden and restaurant on the Spree in the government quarter, next to the Chancellery. The beer garden runs April–September, 11:00–21:30 ([in-berlin-brandenburg](https://www.in-berlin-brandenburg.com/Location/Biergarten/Zollpackhof.html)). Listed among the [Top10 Berlin beer gardens](https://www.top10berlin.de/de/cat/essen-267/biergaerten-386/zollpackhof-biergarten-54).
