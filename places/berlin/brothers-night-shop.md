# Brother's Night Shop

## Hours

- Mon–Fri: 10:00–01:00

## Notes

Späti near the bottom of Schönhauser Allee with tables outside ([easyJet Traveller](https://www.easyjet.com/en/traveller/germany/berlin/one-stop-shop-popping-the-cap-on-berlins-sensational-spati-culture)); hosts the nightly "Stranger Chess" meetup ([The Berliner](https://www.the-berliner.com/berlin/midnight-chess-prenzlauer-berg-brothers-night-shop/)); also a UPS access point.
