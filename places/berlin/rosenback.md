# Rosenback

## Hours

- Mon–Thu: 07:00–00:00
- Fri, Sat: 07:00–03:00
- Sun: 09:00–00:00

## Notes

Bakery-Späti on Rosenthaler Platz with benches outside; this is the Weinbergsweg Späti that [The Culture Trip](https://theculturetrip.com/europe/germany/berlin/articles/berlins-spati-culture) recommends for a cheap beer and people-watching, and [Welcome to Berlin](https://www.welcome-to-berlin.com/de/berlin-lifestyle/berlin-tipps/923-spaeti-beim-rosenthaler-platz-in-berlin) also features it.

- Hours: [Wolt](https://wolt.com/en/deu/berlin/venue/rosenback), October 2026; some directories (e.g. [Späti Finder](https://www.xn--sptifinder-r5a.de/detail/60/rosenback)) list it as open 24 hours; Wolt times may be delivery hours.
