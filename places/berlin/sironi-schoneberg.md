# Sironi (Schöneberg)

## Hours

- Mon–Sun: 08:30–18:00

## Notes

Bakery and café, with a pizzeria ("Sironi La Pizza") open evenings on the same site; card payment only. Sironi's bread is listed in [Der Feinschmecker's bakery guide](https://www.feinschmecker.de/baecker/de/berlin/sironi---il-pane-di-milano-15).

- Hours: [Sironi](https://sironi.de/), October 2026; café hours. Pizzeria: Mon–Fri 17:00–23:00, Sat–Sun 13:00–23:00. An older Sironi page gave the café 08:00–19:00.
