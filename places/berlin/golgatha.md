## Notes

Beer garden in Viktoriapark. Summer season runs April–September; from October to March, opening depends on the weather. After 22:00, use the main entrance on Katzbachstraße because the Dudenstraße entrance is closed ([in-berlin-brandenburg](https://www.in-berlin-brandenburg.com/Location/Biergarten/Golgatha.html)). Listed among the [Top10 Berlin beer gardens](https://www.top10berlin.de/de/cat/essen-267/biergaerten-386/biergarten-golgatha).

- Hours: [in-berlin-brandenburg](https://www.in-berlin-brandenburg.com/Location/Biergarten/Golgatha.html), October 2026; listed as Mon–Fri: 12:00–late; Sat, Sun: 11:00–late (no closing time published). These are April–September hours. From October to March, opening depends on the weather and there are no fixed hours.
