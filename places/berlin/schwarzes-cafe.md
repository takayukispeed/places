## Notes

Open since 1978 per [Schwarzes Café](https://schwarzescafe-berlin.de/), near Savignyplatz. A weekday lunch menu runs Mon–Fri 11:30–15:00 per the [Mittagstisch page](https://schwarzescafe-berlin.de/mittagstisch/). Reservations need at least two days' notice by email to reservierung@schwarzescafe-berlin.de, or phone for short notice.
