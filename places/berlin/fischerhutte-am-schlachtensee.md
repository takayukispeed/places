## Notes

Far from the centre in the southwest, on the Schlachtensee. A lakeside beer garden that opens daily from 09:00, with the restaurant from 12:00 ([rausgegangen](https://rausgegangen.de/en/locations/fischerhutte-am-schlachtensee/)). Listed among [Top10 Berlin's smart waterside restaurants](https://www.top10berlin.de/de/cat/essen-267/schicke-restaurants-am-wasser-491/fischerhuette-am-schlachtensee-140). Oktoberfest runs 25 September – 17 October 2026 ([visitBerlin](https://www.visitberlin.de/en/node/2290006)).
