# Café am Neuen See

## Hours

- Mon–Fri: 12:00–22:00
- Sat, Sun: 11:00–22:00

## Notes

Lakeside beer garden and restaurant in the Tiergarten; the beer garden runs in good weather, while the indoor restaurant stays open year-round ([Top10 Berlin](https://www.top10berlin.de/de/cat/essen-267/biergaerten-386/cafe-am-neuen-see-1071)). Reviewed by [The Infatuation](https://www.theinfatuation.com/berlin/reviews/cafe-am-neuen-see). Oktoberfest runs Fridays and Saturdays 17:00–23:00 from 25 September to 17 October 2026 ([berlin.de](https://www.berlin.de/en/events/8459264-2842498-oktoberfest-at-the-cafe-am-neuen-see.en.html)).

- Hours: [berlin.de](https://www.berlin.de/restaurants/biergarten/9291780-3621819-cafe-am-neuen-see.html), October 2026; beer garden opens only in good weather. Top10 Berlin lists the café as daily 09:00–00:00.
