# Prater Garten

## Hours

- Mon–Fri: 12:00–00:00
- Sat, Sun: 10:00–00:00

## Notes

Berlin's oldest beer garden, on Kastanienallee; listed by [berlin.de](https://www.berlin.de/sehenswuerdigkeiten/3561216-3558930-prater.html) and among [1000things' best beer gardens in Berlin](https://www.1000thingsmagazine.com/de/a/1595050/biergaerten-berlin/). Address is Kastanienallee 7–9.

- Hours: [Mes Prestiges](https://mesprestiges.com/en/berlin/places/prater-garten), October 2026; beer garden is seasonal (roughly April–October) and weather-dependent; berlin.de lists the indoor Gaststätte from 17:00 Mon–Sat and 12:00 Sun. Sources conflict.
