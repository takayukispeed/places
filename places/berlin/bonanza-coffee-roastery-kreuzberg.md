# Bonanza Coffee Roastery (Kreuzberg)

## Hours

- Mon–Fri: 09:00–18:00
- Sat, Sun: 10:00–18:00

## Notes

Roastery café in a courtyard off Adalbertstraße, next to the Kinderbauernhof Am Mauerplatz, covered by [Sprudge](https://sprudge.com/bonanza-coffee-kreuzberg-122704.html). Bonanza is named among the top specialty cafés in [Berlin Tourism's coffee guide](https://www.berlintourism.org/berlin-coffee-cafes/).

- Hours: [Bonanza Coffee](https://bonanzacoffee.de/pages/where-to-find), October 2026; top10 Berlin lists it as cash only.
