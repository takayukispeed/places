# The Barn (Neukölln)

## Hours

- Mon–Fri: 10:00–18:00
- Sat, Sun: 11:00–18:00

## Notes

The Barn was named Crema Magazin's "Roaster of the Year 2025" per [Top10 Berlin](https://www.top10berlin.de/en/cat/eating-257/cafes-coffee-fans-2797/the-barn), and it leads [Berlin Tourism's coffee guide](https://www.berlintourism.org/berlin-coffee-cafes/)'s list of top specialty cafés. The Barn has about ten Berlin cafés (plus Ku'damm, Kranzler Eck, Potsdamer Platz, Weinhaus Huth, Checkpoint Charlie, and the roastery on Voltastraße); only four central ones are listed here.

- Hours: [The Barn](https://thebarn.de/pages/locations/cafe-neukolln), October 2026; conflicting sources: European Coffee Trip lists 10:00–18:00 daily, and Kauperts lists closing at 17:00.
