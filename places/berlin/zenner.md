# Zenner

## Hours

- Mon–Sun: 11:00–22:00

## Notes

Historic beer garden on the Spree at Treptower Park with about 1,500 seats. It opens daily in summer and on fine weekends out of season ([rausgegangen](https://rausgegangen.de/en/pages/zenner-bier-und-weingarten/)). From mid-November to early February it turns into a winter garden ([berlin.de](https://www.berlin.de/en/tourism/insider-tips/beer-gardens/9241107-9086094-zenner.en.html)). Featured in [tipBerlin's beer garden guide](https://www.tip-berlin.de/food/trinken-gehen/biergaerten-berlin/).

- Hours: [berlinstadtservice](https://www.berlinstadtservice.de/restaurants/biergarten/Zenner_Biergarten.html), October 2026; hours vary with the weather; out of season it opens only on fine weekends. Berliner Zeitung (undated) lists Mon–Wed 12:00–19:00, Thu to 20:00, Fri to 21:00, and Sat–Sun 11:00–21:00.
