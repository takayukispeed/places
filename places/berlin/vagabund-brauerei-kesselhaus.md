## Notes

Brewery and taproom in the Osram-Höfe; the original Antwerpener Straße taproom closed after the brewery's insolvency, and operations continue at the Kesselhaus ([From Place to Place](https://fromplacetoplace.travel/germany/berlin/vagabund-brewery/)). Listed in [Top10 Berlin](https://www.top10berlin.de/en/cat/eating-257/berlin-breweries-2974/vagabund-brauerei-kesselhaus)'s Berlin breweries.
