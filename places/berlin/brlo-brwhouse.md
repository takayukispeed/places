## Notes

Berlin craft brewery's shipping-container brewhouse and beer garden at Park am Gleisdreieck; its [official page](https://en.brlo.de/gastronomien/brlo-brwhouse) calls it an LGBTQIA+ safe space, and it appears in [BerlinEcho's craft beer guide](https://berlinecho.de/craft-beer-berlin-brauereien/).
