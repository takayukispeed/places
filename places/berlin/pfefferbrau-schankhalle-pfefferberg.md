# Schankhalle Pfefferberg (Pfefferbräu)

## Hours

- Sun–Thu: 16:00–23:00
- Fri, Sat: 15:00–23:00

## Notes

Brewpub with a beer garden under old trees. In summer, on 'Berg-Sonntag' and weather permitting, guests can tap their own beer ([berlin.de](https://www.berlin.de/en/tourism/insider-tips/beer-gardens/10335841-9086094-schankhalle-pfefferberg.en.html)). Listed among [Top10 Berlin's brewhouses](https://www.top10berlin.de/de/cat/essen-267/berliner-brauhaeuser-1483/schankhalle-pfefferberg-pfefferberaeu-4054).

- Hours: [visitBerlin](https://www.visitberlin.de/fr/node/22106), October 2026; sources conflict. Top10 Berlin (October 2024) lists Tue–Sat 16:00–22:30, closed Sun and Mon.
