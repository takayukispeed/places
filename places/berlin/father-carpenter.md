# Father Carpenter

## Hours

- Mon–Fri: 09:00–16:00
- Sat, Sun: 10:00–17:00

## Notes

Coffee and brunch café in a courtyard (Hof 1) off Münzstraße, open since 2015; named among the top specialty cafés in [Berlin Tourism's coffee guide](https://www.berlintourism.org/berlin-coffee-cafes/) and featured by [Barista Magazine](https://www.baristamagazine.com/tag/father-carpenter/).

- Hours: [European Coffee Trip](https://europeancoffeetrip.com/cafe/fathercarpenter-berlin/), October 2026; conflicting sources: findit.city lists Sunday as 12:00–16:00, and Foodle lists closing at 16:00 on weekends.
