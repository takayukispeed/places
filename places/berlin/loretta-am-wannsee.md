## Notes

Far from the centre, near S-Bahn Wannsee. A long-running beer garden that closed after 14 years and reopened under new operator Sascha Siebenrock ([Berliner Kurier](https://www.berliner-kurier.de/berlin/loretta-am-wannsee-wieder-da-sie-ist-ganz-die-alte-li.2233602)). visitBerlin lists an Oktoberfest there for September–October 2026 ([visitBerlin](https://www.visitberlin.de/en/node/2290004)).
