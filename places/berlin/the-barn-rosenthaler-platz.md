# The Barn (Rosenthaler Platz)

## Hours

- Mon–Fri: 08:00–18:00
- Sat, Sun: 10:00–18:00

## Notes

Described as a new café on the official page. The Barn was named Crema Magazin's "Roaster of the Year 2025" per [Top10 Berlin](https://www.top10berlin.de/en/cat/eating-257/cafes-coffee-fans-2797/the-barn), and it leads [Berlin Tourism's coffee guide](https://www.berlintourism.org/berlin-coffee-cafes/)'s list of top specialty cafés. The Barn has about ten Berlin cafés (plus Ku'damm, Kranzler Eck, Potsdamer Platz, Weinhaus Huth, Checkpoint Charlie, and the roastery on Voltastraße); only four central ones are listed here.

- Hours: [The Barn](https://thebarn.de/pages/locations/rosenthaler-platz), October 2026; kauperts lists Sunday opening at 11:00.
