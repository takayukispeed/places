# FREA Bakery

## Hours

- Mon–Sun: 08:00–17:00

## Notes

Zero-waste, plant-based sourdough bakery offshoot of FREA restaurant (Torstraße). [The Berliner](https://www.the-berliner.com/food/best-bakeries-in-berlin/) says its croissants are unusually flaky despite being made without butter; also [listed by Falstaff](https://www.falstaff.com/en/cafes/frea-bakery).
