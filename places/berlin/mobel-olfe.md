# Möbel Olfe

## Hours

- Mon: Closed
- Tue–Sat: 18:00–03:00
- Sun: Closed

## Notes

Queer-friendly Kneipe in a former furniture shop at Kottbusser Tor; entrance on Dresdener Straße (Lonely Planet). Called "der ewige Geheimtipp" by the [Berliner Zeitung](https://www.berliner-zeitung.de/article/die-bar-moebel-olfe-liegt-mitten-im-zentrum-kreuzberg-am-kottbusser-tor-und-hat-das-nachtleben-veraendert-der-ewige-geheimtipp-63702). Thursday is a gay night and Tuesday a lesbian/trans*/inter* night ([Siegessäule](https://www.siegessaeule.de/en/events/bars/olfe-schwulenschubsen-25/2025-09-11/21:30/)).

- Hours: [Top10 Berlin](https://www.top10berlin.de/en/cat/night-life-259/lgbt-bars-2860/moebel-olfe-3991), October 2026; older listings (Time Out, Squat Radar) say open Sunday from 18:00 too; closing time varies by source (Das Örtliche says 03:00).
