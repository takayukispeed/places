## Notes

Sourdough bakery from Florian Domberger; [The Berliner](https://www.the-berliner.com/food/florian-domberger-brot-werk-bread-bakery/) calls it "a must-try when it comes to sourdough, Brezeln or their famous Bavarian Dampfnudeln". Also [interviewed by tip Berlin](https://www.tip-berlin.de/essen-trinken/domberger-brotwerk-moabit-interview/).

- Hours: [Domberger Brot-Werk](https://domberger-brot-werk.com/), October 2026; closed public holidays. Directories give Mon 15:00–18:30 and Tue–Thu to 18:30; the official site also mentions autumn-holiday reduced hours that may not apply to this shop.
