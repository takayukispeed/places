# Albatross Bäckerei

## Hours

- Mon–Sun: 08:00–18:00

## Notes

Sourdough bakery founded in 2016; supplies cafés and restaurants around town. Small shop with no indoor seating, just a bench outside. Named one of [the 10 best bakeries in Berlin by Falstaff](https://www.falstaff.com/en/the-best/the-10-best-bakeries-in-berlin-1), which calls its sourdough very good.
