# Cocolo Ramen (Kreuzberg)

## Hours

- Mon–Sat: 12:00–23:00
- Sun: Closed

## Notes

Ramen shop from The Duc Ngo's Kuchi group; opened 2013 and moved within Kreuzberg in 2019 ([FU Berlin](https://userblogs.fu-berlin.de/forschungswerkstatt-japan/2022/06/29/the-joy-of-fieldwork-interview-at-cocolo-ramen-x-berg/)). Listed in [Falstaff's 10 best ramen restaurants in Berlin](https://www.falstaff.com/en/the-best/the-10-best-ramen-restaurants-in-berlin-1); Berlin Food Stories notes everything is made in-house, "from the miso to the gyoza" ([Berlin Food Stories](https://www.berlinfoodstories.com/map/cocolo-ramen)). No reservations.

- Hours: [Kuchi](https://kuchi.de/restaurant/cocolo-x-berg/), October 2026; last order 22:30. Older listings give the previous address Paul-Lincke-Ufer 39–40 and Sunday evening hours.
