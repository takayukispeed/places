## Notes

Japanese deli with a grocery shop; serves rice bowls, udon and soba noodle soups and sushi per [Berlin Food Stories](https://www.berlinfoodstories.com/reviews/smart-deli). It moved from Chausseestraße 5 to Novalisstraße per [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g187323-d3340093-Reviews-Smart_Deli-Berlin.html).
