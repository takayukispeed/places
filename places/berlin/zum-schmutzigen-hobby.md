## Notes

Queer bar founded by Nina Queer, in a former fire station on the RAW-Gelände; on [Top10 Berlin's LGBTIQ* scene bars](https://www.top10berlin.de/de/cat/nachtleben-269/szene-bars-fuer-schwule-und-lesben-1815/bar-zum-schmutzigen-hobby-1639) and [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/berlin/lgbtq/zum-schmutzigen-hobby). Runs weekly drag bingo with the Wodkasisters ([Siegessäule](https://www.siegessaeule.de/en/events/bars/zshobby-bingo-wodkasisters/2026-07-01/20:00/)).
