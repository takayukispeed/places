## Notes

Sichuan xiaomian (Chongqing-style noodles) from chef Ash Lee, started as pop-ups; [Mit Vergnügen](https://mitvergnuegen.com/2019/chungking-noodles-kreuzberg/) called them "die schärfsten Nudeln der Stadt". Also reviewed by [The Infatuation](https://www.theinfatuation.com/berlin/reviews/chungking-noodles). Waitlist in the evenings.
