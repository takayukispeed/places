# Bar Tartare

## Hours

- Sun, Mon: Closed
- Tue, Wed: 17:00–23:00
- Thu–Sat: 17:00–00:00

## Notes

Natural wine bar that takes over the [Birds & The Beets](https://scoutmagazine.ca/venue/bar-tartare/) café space in the evenings; it opened in October 2023. There is no wine list: bottles are picked from a fridge with prices written on them, and food comes from rotating guest chefs ([The Infatuation](https://www.theinfatuation.com/vancouver/reviews/bar-tartare)). Named an Editors' Choice wine bar in [Vancouver Magazine's 2026 Restaurant Awards](https://vanmag.com/?p=49332). The patio is reportedly first come, first served. It is in Gastown, not on Main St.
