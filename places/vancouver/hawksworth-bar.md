## Notes

In the Rosewood Hotel Georgia; the restaurant has three rooms including the cocktail bar and lounge ([Site](https://hawksworthrestaurant.com/)). Cocktail hour daily 16:00–18:00, limited seating, lounge only ([Site](https://hawksworthrestaurant.com/contact/)). Held a 15th-anniversary dinner in May 2026 ([Hawksworth](https://hawksworth.ca/2026/05/06/hawksworth-restaurant-celebrates-15-year-anniversary-with-the-origins-dinner-with-legendary-guest-chefs-michel-jacob-and-pino-posteraro/)). The hotel also has a separate lobby cocktail bar, The Georgia Bar ([Rosewood](https://www.rosewoodhotels.com/en/hotel-georgia-vancouver/dining/the-georgia-bar)).
