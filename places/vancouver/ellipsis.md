## Notes

Unit 205 in Arthur Erickson's Waterfall Building near Granville Island. It is a "dual bar" for coffee and cocktails from owner Ming Yang that launched in July 2025 ([Retail Insider](https://retail-insider.com/retail-insider/2025/07/coffee-cocktails-and-community-ellipsis-set-to-launch-in-vancouver-photos/)). It is a café by day, with share plates at dinner ([Tock](https://www.exploretock.com/ellipsis-vancouver/)). The room seats up to 47, designed by SML Studio Architecture ([Dezeen](https://www.dezeen.com/2025/10/11/ellipsis-cafe-bar-waterfall-building-vancouver-sml-studio-architecture/)). Tock bookings take up to six people; walk-ins are welcome when space allows.
