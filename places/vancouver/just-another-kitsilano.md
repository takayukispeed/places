# Just Another (Kitsilano)

## Hours

- Sun–Wed: 07:00–17:00
- Thu–Sat: 07:00–22:00

## Notes

A café by day; on Thursday to Saturday it becomes a wine bar with table service and small plates, per the [official site](https://www.justanother.ca/kitsilano). Formerly named Brightside, per [Vancouver Is Awesome](https://www.vancouverisawesome.com/food-and-drink/just-another-name-change-kits-all-day-cafe-assumes-old-identity-7089565).
