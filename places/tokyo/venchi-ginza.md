# Venchi (Ginza)

## Hours

- Daily: 09:00–21:00

## Notes

Italian chocolate and gelato maker; this was its first boutique in Japan (opened December 2019) and reopened June 24, 2026 as a "Chocogelateria" flagship ([Have a Good Holiday](https://haveagood-holiday.com/en/articles/venchi-ginza-flagship-store-renewal-2026)). Other branches exist across Tokyo, including Shinjuku Takashimaya ([Precious](https://precious.jp/articles/-/20914)).
