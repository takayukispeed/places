# Meikyoku Kissa Lion

## Hours

- Daily: 11:00–22:30

## Notes

Classical music café founded in 1926 (rebuilt 1950) with towering custom speakers and a hushed, no-photos rule; listed in [Table Journey's Tokyo listening bars](https://tablejourney.com/japan/tokyo/nightlife/listening-bars/).

- Hours: [Macaroni](https://macaro-ni.jp/23838), October 2026; last order 22:20. Closed Jan 1–4 and five days after Obon. Scheduled concerts daily at 15:00 and 19:00. Coffee and tea only, no alcohol.
