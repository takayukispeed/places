# Eight Coffee (Shinjuku)

## Hours

- Mon–Fri: 07:00–19:00
- Sat, Sun: 08:00–19:00

## Notes

Small, minimal coffee stand about a minute from Shinjuku-gyoemmae Station ([Daily Shinjuku](https://daily-shinjuku.tokyo/2019/04/07/shinjukugyoen-cafe-eightcoffee/); [Retty](https://retty.me/area/PRE13/ARE1/SUB105/100001463307/)). There is also a branch near Aoyama-itchome ([This Tokyo](https://thistokyo.com/ja/minato-ku/eight-coffee/)).

- Hours: [4travel](https://4travel.jp/dm_shisetsu/11768039), October 2026; listings agree on these hours and say it opens every day, but suggest checking its Instagram for changes. Holidays follow weekend hours.
- Pin: approximate, to the chōme (block group) of the address.
