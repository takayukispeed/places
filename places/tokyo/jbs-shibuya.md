# JBS (Jazz Blues Soul)

## Hours

- Daily: 14:00–23:00

## Notes

Tiny second-floor listening bar where owner Kobayashi spins from a collection of over 10,000 jazz, blues and soul records ([The Vinyl Factory](https://thevinylfactory.com/features/jbs-jazz-bar-a-vinyl-sanctuary-in-the-midst-of-tokyo/)). Ranked in [Table Journey's Tokyo listening bars list](https://tablejourney.com/japan/tokyo/nightlife/listening-bars/), which notes there is "no cover". Second floor.
