# Little Nap Coffee Stand

## Hours

- Mon–Fri: 09:00–18:00
- Sat, Sun: 09:00–19:00

## Notes

Listed by [Time Out Tokyo](https://www.timeout.jp/tokyo/ja/レストラン/リトルナップコーヒースタンド) as a stand across from Yoyogi Park since 2011. Small coffee stand (9 seats) known for its banana bread. A separate Little Nap Coffee Roasters operates in Tomigaya.

- Hours: [Tabelog](https://tabelog.com/en/tokyo/A1318/A131810/13123533/), October 2026; other sources list Monday closures or 19:00 weekday closing; check before going.
