- Hours: [Jazz in Japan](https://www.jazzinjapan.com/clubs-and-venues/no-room-for-squares), October 2026; Weekdays 16:00–02:00; weekend hours vary with live shows (some Sunday afternoon sets). Entry fee for some live events.
