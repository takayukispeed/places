# DJ Bar Bridge

## Hours

- Daily: 20:00–05:00

## Notes

Tenth-floor DJ bar overlooking Shibuya Crossing with a Rey Audio sound system, listed by [Time Out Tokyo](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/bars-and-pubs/dj-bar-bridge) and in [Roadbook's best listening bars in Tokyo](https://roadbook.com/tokyo/city-guide/best-listening-bars-live-music-venues-clubs-nightlife-tokyo/) (updated March 2026). Parkside Kyodo Building 10F. A sister bar exists in [Shinjuku](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/bars-and-pubs/dj-bar-bridge-shinjuku).

- Hours: [Time Out Tokyo](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/bars-and-pubs/dj-bar-bridge), October 2026; Entry about ¥1,000 per Time Out; smoking in a separated area.
- Pin: approximate, to the chōme (block group) of the address.
