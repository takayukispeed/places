# Bar Bonobo

## Hours

## Notes

Two-storey old house near Kitasando with vintage Altec speakers and a tatami floor upstairs; Roadbook calls it "a bar, club and gallery experience under one roof" in its [best listening bars in Tokyo](https://roadbook.com/tokyo/city-guide/best-listening-bars-live-music-venues-clubs-nightlife-tokyo/) (updated March 2026). Also listed by [Time Out Tokyo](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/clubs/bar-bonobo).

- Hours: [Time Out Tokyo](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/clubs/bar-bonobo), October 2026; hours vary by event; parties mostly Wed–Sat from about 22:00, often until 05:00, with door entry around ¥2,000 including a drink.
- Pin: approximate, to the chōme (block group) of the address.
