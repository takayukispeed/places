# Ginza Music Bar

## Hours

- Mon–Sat: 18:00–04:00
- Sun: Closed

## Notes

Founded in 2014 by DJ/producer Shinichi Osawa and coffee producer Nobuhiro Toriba, with 3,000+ records on Garrard turntables and Tannoy Westminster speakers; listed in [Mr Porter's world's best listening bars](https://www.mrporter.com/en-jp/journal/lifestyle/best-listening-bars-25202734) and on [Time Out Tokyo](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/nightlife/ginza-music-bar). Brown Place Building 4F. A Kyoto branch opened in 2025.
