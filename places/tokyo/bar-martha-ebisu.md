# Bar Martha

## Hours

## Notes

Vinyl listening bar with around 6,000 records played on vintage Tannoy GRF speakers and a strict no-talking, no-photos rule, called "the canonical vinyl listening bar" by [Table Journey](https://tablejourney.com/japan/tokyo/nightlife/listening-bars/). Also featured by [Goop](https://goop.com/place/japan/tokyo/shibuya-bars-and-nightlife/bar-martha/). Behind an unmarked door at the rear of Vera Heights Ebisu, 1F.

- Hours: [Table Journey](https://tablejourney.com/japan/tokyo/nightlife/listening-bars/), October 2026; Evening opening (from 19:00) is consistent, but closing time varies by source (02:00, 03:00 or 05:00); a cover charge applies and reservations are generally not accepted.
- Pin: approximate, to the chōme (block group) of the address.
