# Record Bar Analog

## Hours

- Daily: 20:00–03:00

## Notes

Request-friendly record bar with vintage Altec speakers and a Garrard 301 turntable, picked by [Lonely Planet's best listening bars in Japan](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/articles/best-listening-bars-japan) (May 2026) for having "the plushest seats around". Also on [Time Out Tokyo](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/bars-and-pubs/analog). 3F, across from Mikkeller Tokyo.
