# Bar Music

## Hours

- Mon: Closed
- Tue–Sun: 18:00–02:00

## Notes

Opened in 2010 by DJ and selector Tomoaki Nakamura (formerly of Café Après-midi), playing "everything from jazz to Brazilian music" per [Time Out Tokyo](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/bars-and-pubs/bar-music), which also includes it in its [listening bars roundup](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/nightlife/top-music-bars-in-tokyo). 5F.
