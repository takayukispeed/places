## Hours

## Notes

Original shop of the brand founded by 2014 World AeroPress Champion Shuichi Sasaki, per [Tokyo Weekender](https://www.tokyoweekender.com/food-and-drink/new-tokyo-coffee-shop/). Ground floor of Taishodo Building, about 5 minutes from JR Tamachi. Other branches exist (Ichigaya, Nihonbashi).
