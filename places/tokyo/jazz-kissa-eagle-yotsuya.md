## Notes

Legendary jazz kissa (いーぐる) with a no-talking rule during the day before turning into a jazz bar in the evening, profiled by [DeepTokyo](https://deeptokyo.jp/en/spot/food_gourmet/837/) as "Legendary Jazz Kissa for Enthusiasts and Newcomers Alike". Basement.

- Hours: [Jazz in Japan](https://www.jazzinjapan.com/clubs-and-venues/eagle), October 2026; Closed on public holidays; quiet listening until about 18:00; cash only per one guide.
- Pin: approximate, to the chōme (block group) of the address.
