# Grandfather's

## Hours

- Mon: Closed
- Tue–Sun: 17:00–02:00

## Notes

Called "the current daddy of the city's vintage music bar scene" by [Time Out Tokyo](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/bars-and-pubs/grandfathers); the owner plays 1970s pop, rock and soul from vinyl. Also listed by [Concrete Playground](https://concreteplayground.com/perth/bars/grandfathers-bar-tokyo-shibuya) (updated December 2024). Flat Building B1F.

- Hours: [Time Out Tokyo](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/bars-and-pubs/grandfathers), October 2026; Metropolis lists it as open daily 17:00–03:00 instead; check before visiting.
