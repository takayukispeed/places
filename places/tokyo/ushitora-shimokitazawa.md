# Ushitora

## Hours

- Daily: 11:30–02:00

## Notes

Tap bar for Tochigi's Ushitora Brewery, listed by [Time Out Tokyo](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/bars-and-pubs/ushitora) and [Tokyo Cheapo](https://tokyocheapo.com/restaurant/beer-bar-ushitora); two bars (One, sit-down; Two, standing) with about 30–35 taps between them. 2F.

- Hours: [Time Out Tokyo](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/bars-and-pubs/ushitora), October 2026; hours are for Ushitora One; lunch until 16:00, no lunch Tuesdays. Ushitora Two: Tue–Thu, Sun 15:00–23:30, Fri, Sat 17:00–02:00, closed Mon. Metropolis lists Ushitora One as 17:00–02:00 daily.
- Pin: approximate, to the chōme (block group) of the address.
