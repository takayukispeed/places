# T.Y. Harbor

## Hours

- Daily: 11:30–22:00

## Notes

Waterfront brewpub making Californian-style ales and porters for over 20 years, listed by [Time Out Tokyo](https://timeout.com/tokyo/bars-and-pubs/t-y-harbor) and [Tokyo Weekender](https://www.tokyoweekender.com/food-and-drink/5-brewpubs-where-you-can-explore-craft-beer-in-tokyo/). About 6 minutes from Tennozu Isle Station.

- Hours: [Metropolis](https://metropolisjapan.com/ty-harbor/), October 2026; sources conflict: Japan Travel lists weekdays 11:30–14:00, 17:30–23:30 and weekends 11:30–23:30. Busy evenings and weekends.
