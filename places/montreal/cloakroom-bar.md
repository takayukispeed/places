# Cloakroom Bar

## Hours

- Sun–Thu: 16:00–01:00
- Fri, Sat: 16:00–03:00

## Notes

Speakeasy of about 25 seats, hidden behind a tailor's shop. It has no printed menu; cocktails are made to order ([Tastet](https://tastet.ca/en/reviews/cloakroom-bar-downtown-montreal/?region=mtl), [En Primeur Club](https://www.enprimeurclub.com/bars/cloakroom-montreal-bar)). No reservations: it's first come, first served, with no groups over six.

- Hours: [ma.to](https://ma.to/venue/thecloakroombar), October 2026. [Tastet](https://tastet.ca/en/reviews/cloakroom-bar-downtown-montreal/?region=mtl) has Sunday closing at midnight.
