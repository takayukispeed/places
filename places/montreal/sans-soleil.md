# Sans Soleil

## Hours

- Mon–Tue: 18:30–01:00
- Wed–Sun: 18:30–03:00

## Notes

Speakeasy in the basement of Fleurs & Cadeaux. Japanese-inspired food and cocktails, vinyl-only music; it turns into more of a club late at night.
