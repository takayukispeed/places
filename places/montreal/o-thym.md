# O'Thym

## Hours

- Daily: 17:30–22:00

## Notes

Bring-your-own-wine bistro in the Village. Also serves weekend brunch ([MTL.org](https://www.mtl.org/en/experience/where-eat-montreal-village)).

- Address: 1257 Rue Atateken ([Yelp](https://www.yelp.ca/biz/o-thym-montr%C3%A9al-2), September 2026). Older listings give 1112 Boulevard de Maisonneuve Est.
- Hours: [Yelp](https://www.yelp.ca/biz/o-thym-montr%C3%A9al-2), September 2026, and [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g155032-d783513-Reviews-O_Thym-Montreal_Quebec.html). Tripadvisor also lists weekend brunch, 10:00–14:00.
- Reservations: books through Libro, with bar and table seating (seen on its Google Maps listing, October 2026). The Reserve button goes to its website; Google Maps' Reserve button opens the Libro booking directly.
