# Éléonore

## Hours

- Mon, Tue: Closed
- Wed–Sat: 17:00–22:00
- Sun: Closed

## Notes

French buvette near Frontenac metro, with modern French sharing plates, a wine list and a seasonal menu ([Tastet](https://tastet.ca/en/reviews/eleonore-contemporary-french-cuisine-with-a-modern-twist/)). Sister restaurant of Junco, a few doors away ([La Presse](https://www.lapresse.ca/gourmand/restaurants/2025-01-30/eleonore-et-junco-ca-cartonne-rue-ontario.php)). Small room, so booking is strongly recommended.
