## Notes

Neighbourhood pub with house brews, Québec microbrewery beers, flatbreads, a pool table and a terrace ([MTL.org](https://www.mtl.org/en/what-to-do/nightlife/yisst)).

- Hours: [RestoMontréal](https://www.restomontreal.ca/resto/yisst-montreal/9924/en/) and [Schlouk Map](https://www.schlouk-map.com/en/places/yisst). Yelp has it closed Sundays and open to 3:00 Thursday to Saturday.
