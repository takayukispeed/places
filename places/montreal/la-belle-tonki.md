# La Belle Tonki

## Hours

- Mon–Tue: Closed
- Wed–Thu: 17:00–22:00
- Fri–Sat: 17:00–23:00
- Sun: 17:00–22:00

## Notes

Asian comfort food (phở, Asian poutine and burgers) with cocktails and local beer; manga decor and karaoke nights ([Tastet](https://tastet.ca/en/reviews/la-belle-tonki-pho-and-poutine-under-one-roof/)).

- Hours: [labelletonki.ca](https://www.labelletonki.ca/).
- Reservations: first come, first served, except groups of six or more ([its website](https://www.labelletonki.ca/a-propos)). Arrive before 18:00 or after 20:00 to avoid the rush.
