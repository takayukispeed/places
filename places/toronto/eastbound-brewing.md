# Eastbound Brewing

## Hours

## Notes

Locally owned brewpub that brews all its beer on-site; a [Tex-Mex kitchen (Eastbound Cantina)](https://eastboundbeer.com/pages/about) launched in 2024. It has a [Queen Street patio](https://www.exploretock.com/eastbound-brewing-company-toronto/). Kitchen closed Mondays; Sunday brunch 10:00–14:00.
