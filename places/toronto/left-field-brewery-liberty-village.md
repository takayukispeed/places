# Left Field Brewery (Liberty Village)

## Hours

- Mon–Sun: 11:00–00:00

## Notes

Suite G1; the entrance is in the back courtyard by Liberty St. Bi-level taproom opened in November 2023 with [16 draught lines and 29 TVs](https://canadianbeernews.com/2023/11/20/left-field-brewery-opens-new-location-in-torontos-liberty-village), a kitchen and a cold beer store. Reservations for groups up to 8; 70% of seats are kept for walk-ins.

- Hours: [Left Field Brewery](https://www.leftfieldbrewery.ca/contact-us), October 2026; cold beer store closes at 23:00. Some listings show Mon–Thu and Sun closing at 23:00.
