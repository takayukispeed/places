## Notes

The original 2008 Pizzeria Libretto reopened as Bar Libretto on 20 February 2026: bar-focused, with a pared-back menu that still serves Neapolitan pizza ([blogTO](https://www.blogto.com/eat_drink/2026/02/pizza-libretto-ossington-toronto), [Streets of Toronto](https://streetsoftoronto.com/food/restaurants/this-pizza-spot-just-became-the-coolest-late-night-hangout-on-ossington/)). Happy hours 16:00–18:00 and 21:00–23:00; DJs Friday and Saturday.
