# Steam Whistle Brewing taproom

## Hours

- Mon: 13:00–21:00

- Wed, Thu: 13:00–21:00

## Notes

Brewery taproom in the historic Roundhouse with a [cobblestone patio](https://steamwhistle.ca/blogs/news/oundhouse-summer-patio-menu-toronto) among vintage locomotives overlooking Roundhouse Park. Walk-ins only for 3 hours before Blue Jays games.

- Hours: [Steam Whistle](https://steamwhistle.ca/pages/contact), October 2026; hours may vary for private events and by season. Yelp (October 2026) and Apple Maps list roughly 11:00–22:00 (Sun to 20:00 or 21:00). The kitchen keeps separate hours.
