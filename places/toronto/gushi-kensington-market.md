# Gushi (Kensington Market)

## Hours

- Mon: Closed
- Tue–Sat: 11:30–19:00
- Sun: Closed

## Notes

Original Gushi stall in Market 707, the row of food stalls behind the Toronto Public Library branch at Dundas and Bathurst ([blogTO](https://www.blogto.com/restaurants/gushi-food-toronto/)). Known for karaage chicken; also takoyaki and croquettes ([Daily Hive](https://dailyhive.com/toronto/japanese-street-food-toronto)). Picnic tables in front of the market ([Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/471353/gushi-dundas-w-x-bathurst-reopen-march-3rd)).

- Hours: [Yelp](https://www.yelp.ca/biz/gushi-japanese-street-food-toronto), November 2026; other listings differ: Wanderlog says Tue–Sat 11:45–19:00, and online ordering runs 11:45–18:45.
