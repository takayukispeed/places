# Avling Kitchen & Brewery

- Mon: Closed

## Notes

Brewpub with the brewery in the basement and the restaurant on the main floor; much of the produce comes from its [rooftop garden](https://torontolife.com/food/whats-menu-avling-kitchen-brewery-leslievilles-new-brewpub-big-rooftop-garden/). Happy hour Tue–Sun 16:00–18:00 ([Avling](https://www.avling.ca/menu)). The on-site retail shop keeps [separate hours](https://shop.avling.ca/pages/retail-store-hours-of-operation).

- Hours: [Avling](https://www.avling.ca/menu), October 2026; Official pages conflict: the homepage hours section lists dinner 17:00–21:00 Tue–Sun, the menu page lists 17:00–22:00 (Fri–Sat 23:00), and brunch is listed as ending at 15:00 or 15:30. OpenTable shows different hours again.
