# Gale's Snack Bar

## Hours

- Mon–Fri: 10:00–18:00
- Sat: 12:00–17:00
- Sun: Closed

## Notes

Recognized in [Narcity's list of the best cheap eats in Toronto](https://www.narcity.com/toronto/toronto-local-definitive-list-best-cheap-eats-in-the-city). Family-run diner at Eastern and Carlaw; the same family has run it for about six decades ([Daily Hive](https://dailyhive.com/toronto/gales-toronto-1960s-era-diner)). Cash only. Building stood in for Christopher Walken's joke shop in *Hairspray* (2007).

- Hours: [NOW Toronto](https://nowtoronto.com/food-and-drink/snack-attack-at-gales/), October 2026; cash only. Hours come from an old review; Daily Hive says the diner doesn't keep a strict schedule these days. No phone.
