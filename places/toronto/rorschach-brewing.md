## Notes

Brewpub in a [100-year-old mansion](https://www.blog.goodhood.ca/whats-good/rorschach-brewing-brings-subjective-beer-experience-to-east-end-leslieville-beaches-brewpub) with a taproom, dining room and second-floor patio. Kitchen hours are shorter (Mon–Tue from 17:00, Wed–Thu from 14:00) per its [FAQ](https://www.rorschachbrewing.com/pages/faqs). There is a second taproom at 815 Kipling Ave in Etobicoke, outside the city core.
