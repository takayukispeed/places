# Oidé Coffee

## Hours

- Mon: Closed
- Tue–Sun: 08:00–16:00

## Notes

Run by Kento and Rika Kitayama (formerly of Iktsuarpok in Gastown). Since 2023 it has been in Arthur Erickson's Waterfall Building near Granville Island; the drinks menu is coffee "with milk or without" ([Daily Hive](https://dailyhive.com/vancouver/oide-coffee-vancouver)). It has signed a lease for a separate coffee lab and roastery and aimed to start roasting in spring/summer 2026 ([Daily Hive](https://dailyhive.com/vancouver/oide-coffee-lab-vancouver)). It is in the same building as Ellipsis.
