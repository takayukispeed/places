# Bar Gobo

## Hours

- Mon, Tue: Closed
- Wed–Sun: 17:00–23:00

## Notes

Hi-fi wine bar with a turntable at the centre of a roughly 25-seat room, plus a Filipino-influenced menu ([The Infatuation](https://www.theinfatuation.com/vancouver/reviews/bar-gobo)). It grew out of a pop-up at Burdock & Co and took over the former Tuck Shoppe / The Parker space ([Scout](https://scoutmagazine.ca/bar-gobo-opening-soon-on-union-street/)). Listed as Michelin Guide Recommended (2024) on [OpenTable](https://www.opentable.com/r/bar-gobo-vancouver). It is on the Chinatown/Strathcona edge.

- Hours: [Source](https://www.opentable.com/r/bar-gobo-vancouver), October 2026; Scout Magazine lists Wed–Sat 17:00–22:00; happy hour per OpenTable is 17:00–18:00 and 21:00–23:00.
